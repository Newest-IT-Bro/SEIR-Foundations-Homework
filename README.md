## My Commitment: Benjamin Cooper

I'm Benjamin Cooper, and I'm in for the full year. Yes, the whole year, even the part in February when everyone's motivation quietly dies.

I know this isn't a bootcamp. Nobody is going to hand me a certificate after three weekends and a pizza party and call me an engineer. This program puts me on real infrastructure, where things break, mistakes cost actual money, and "I'll just restart it" is not a strategy. That's exactly why I signed up.

I've taken the red pill. I swallowed it dry, with no water, and I have regrets about that part. Morpheus told Neo he could only show him the door. My instructors have shown me the door. The door is a terminal with a blinking cursor, and it's judging me.

Neo didn't wake up knowing how to fight. His muscles had never been used. He got tossed around the dojo, faceplanted in the jump program, and the whole crew went "nobody makes the first jump" in that disappointed-dad voice. I fully expect my first jump to go the same way. Terraform will fail and pipelines will go red. Some nights I'll stare at a log for an hour, then whisper "whoa," and then realize I'd been reading the wrong log the whole time. I'm not quitting when that happens. Neo learned by getting punched, and I'll learn from error messages, which hit harder.

Here's what I'm promising myself:

- **I'll keep showing up.** That includes the weeks when nothing works and I start to suspect my laptop is an Agent. Steady effort beats big bursts. Agent Smith never took a day off, and he was the bad guy. Imagine what a good guy could do with that work ethic.

- **I won't skip the hard labs.** When something breaks, I'll read the plan, dig through the logs, and find out why, instead of copying the first Stack Overflow answer and praying. Cypher wanted to forget everything and eat a fake steak. I want to understand everything. And eat a real steak. Both, ideally.

- **I'll trust evidence over hunches.** The Matrix is all about seeing past what looks real. Troubleshooting is the same thing, except the Agents are a missing semicolon and a security group that allows nothing. If I get déjà vu, it means something changed in the system. Probably someone edited prod by hand. Probably me.

- **I'll treat AI as a crew member, not the captain.** Claude through Bedrock, plus my Bash and Python scripts, are like Tank, the operator. Super helpful, loads programs fast, can get me a helicopter pilot manual in seconds. But I've seen all four movies, and I know how it goes when humans hand every decision to the machines. One minute you're asking for a summary, the next you're a battery in a pink goo pod. I check what my tools give me, and the calls stay mine.

- **I'll do things the right way.** Version control, pull requests, and changes I can repeat and explain. No more SSHing into a server at 2am to "just fix it real quick." That's how you end up with a server only the Keymaker can get into, and he's been unavailable since the second movie.

- **I'll build judgment, not just skills.** One day I'll open a terminal and say "I know kung fu." Morpheus will say "show me," and I'll show him a clean `terraform plan` with zero surprises. But knowing kung fu isn't enough. Neo still had to learn when to fight, when to run, and when to just call Trinity. I want to be the engineer who stays calm when production is on fire and everyone in the Slack channel is typing at once.

- **I'll finish.** By the end of this year I want to look at a broken system and see the green code raining down behind it. There is no spoon. There's a misconfigured IAM policy, and I'm the one who's going to fix it.

I'm not looking for shortcuts. The Oracle said being the One is like being in love: nobody can tell you, you just know it. I don't know it yet. But I'll know it by the end of this year, and I won't have needed a cookie to get there. I'll still take the cookie, though.

Free your mind. Then go read the logs.
